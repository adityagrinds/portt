A curious mind with a quiet love for building.

I'm Angel, a second-year Computer Science & Engineering student at Manav Rachna University. I enjoy the whole journey of an idea — the whiteboard chaos, the late-night debugging, and the moment it finally clicks.

As a Google Student Ambassador, I help bring students closer to technology and to each other. At the VisionX hackathon, hosted at the GeeksforGeeks office, my team and I took 1st prize.

I've built through 30 hours at the NASA Space Apps Challenge, learned at AWS Builder Center and Tryst'26, IIT Delhi — and I volunteer teaching children in Rohtak.
